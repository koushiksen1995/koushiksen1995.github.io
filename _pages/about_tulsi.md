Tulsi obtained her Bachelor’s in Medical Studies at ASU (2020-2024) before moving to UofA where she is now pursuing a Physics major with a minor in Mathematics (2025-present). Yes, she has a degree in medicine and is passionate about astronomy!

She is the current Vice President of the Astronomy Club at UofA (2026-2027) and is responsible for general communication between professors, observatories such as Kitt Peak Observatory, and other clubs.

Since Aug 2026, she has been working on detailed modelling of the pulsational properties of massive stars in binaries using the 1D stellar evolution code MESA.
